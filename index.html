About SKN ID

SKN ID is built by a small, passionate team at the intersection of beauty technology and artificial intelligence. Its mission: give every person a skin identity as unique as they are — an alternative to generic, one-size-fits-all skincare advice, for beginners and experienced users alike.

For shoppers, brands and stores

When you share your SKN ID, products, brands and stores can understand what works for you — so shopping becomes more personal, online and in store.
